Sponsor Logistics & Facility Amenities What to Expect on Site
📦 Tabling & Booth Space (Room IL C)
Standard 6ft rectangular table with chairs provided in Room IL C (directly behind registration). Display banners, table runners, marketing collateral, and swag. Tables can stay set up overnight.
🎤 Presentation & A/V (Room IL AB)
Features overhead projection, HDMI input, podium mic, and wireless presentation remote/clicker. Student tech volunteers will assist with laptop setup 10 minutes prior to your talk.
📶 Wi-Fi & Electrical Power
High-speed guest Wi-Fi is available campus-wide via open UIC-Guest portal or eduroam. Standard 120V AC electrical power strips available at tabling stations and podium.
🚗 Parking & Building Access
Public parking is available at the Halsted Street Parking Structure (760 W Taylor St) adjacent to Student Center East. Enter SCE via 750 S Halsted St entrance.
Challenge Track Judging Rubric Recommended Evaluation Criteria
Criteria 1 (25%)
Technical Depth
Algorithmic rigor, circuit depth, proper use of SDKs/APIs, and mathematical correctness.
Criteria 2 (25%)
Challenge Fit
Thorough execution of sponsor's problem statement and fulfillment of all quest requirements.
Criteria 3 (25%)
Innovation
Novelty of approach, creative problem-solving, and practical real-world feasibility or impact.
Criteria 4 (25%)
Presentation
Clarity of demo, well-structured documentation, and ability to defend technical choices.
